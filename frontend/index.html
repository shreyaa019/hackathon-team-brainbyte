Accessibility Features at Mysuru Junction

  • ✔️ Ramps at Main Entrance and Concourse
  • ✔️ Lifts installed at West & East Foot Overbridges
  • ✔️ Tactile floor guiding tiles in main concourse
  • ✔️ Accessible washrooms near Concourse Hall
